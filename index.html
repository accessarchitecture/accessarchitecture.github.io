Access Architecture

The study and design of conditions that influence access to preserved abilities.

Access Architecture is an emerging interdisciplinary field examining why people may possess intact abilities yet experience inconsistent access to them depending on environmental, relational, cognitive, technological, and systemic conditions.

The field draws from disability studies, systems thinking, cognitive science, neurodiversity, human factors, accessibility, and lived experience.

Status

This field is under active development by M. Ian Niad through the Ashfires research ecosystem.

Initial publications, frameworks, and working papers will be released as they are completed.
