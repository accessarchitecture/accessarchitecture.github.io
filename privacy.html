Privacy

A plain-language account of what this site does and does not collect.

What this site collects

Access Architecture does not currently provide accounts, contact forms, newsletter signup, comments, or payments. We do not ask you to submit personal information to read the site.

The homepage uses Google Analytics to understand broad patterns such as visits and page use. Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and may receive information such as a device or browser identifier, approximate location, referral source, and pages visited. We use this information for aggregate site improvement.

Third-party services

Some pages embed YouTube videos using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced host. Loading or playing an embedded video may still involve YouTube processing technical information according to its own policies.

The site also links to external services including PhilPapers, Zenodo, ORCID, W3C, ADA.gov, Section508.gov, and Ashfires Press. Once you leave this site, the destination’s privacy practices apply.

Cookies and choices

Your browser may allow you to block or delete cookies. You can also use browser privacy controls or extensions to limit analytics and third-party content. Blocking these technologies should not prevent you from reading the core text of the site.

Changes and questions

This page may change if the site adds forms, subscriptions, accounts, advertising, or other data practices. The current version is dated August 17, 2026.

For questions about this site or its data practices, contact Ashfires Press.
